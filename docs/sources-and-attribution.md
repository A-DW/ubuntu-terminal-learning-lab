## Available and missing references

The three domain guides contain tool-specific citation markers whose original URL mapping is not available in the supplied files. I treat those references as unresolved, not as working citations.

The shell-redirection companion contains direct links to the [GNU Bash redirection manual](https://www.gnu.org/software/bash/manual/html_node/Redirections.html) and [GNU Bash pipeline manual](https://www.gnu.org/software/bash/manual/html_node/Pipelines.html). These links are present in that note; I have not independently audited every example in this publication step.

I follow these rules when preparing a note for publication:

1. Keep identifiable source links attached to the relevant material.
2. Do not invent URLs, source titles or mappings for old numbered markers.
3. Explain unresolved references without presenting them as verified evidence.
4. Identify later corrections separately from the original draft.
5. Distinguish my explanations, AI-assisted drafting, source-derived material and observed results.
