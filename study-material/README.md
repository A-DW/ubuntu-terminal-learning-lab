## Notes versus exercises

My three domain groupings and companion note are not the same as the six practice blocks. The blocks are my learning design, not a claim about official exam weighting.

Some notes contain examples beyond an individual exercise's safeguards. Follow the exercise's execution labels and target-verification rules rather than treating a general example as permission to modify your own system.

## Reference limitations

The domain guides retain numbered source markers without their original URL mapping. I will not invent citations to make them look complete. The shell companion has identifiable GNU Bash links, documented in [sources and attribution](../docs/sources-and-attribution.md).
